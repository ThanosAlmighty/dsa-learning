# Recursion vs Iteration

## Recursion
### PROS:
* [cite_start]Forces you to follow DRY (Don't Repeat Yourself) principals to reduce duplication[cite: 1].
* [cite_start]Readability is a benefit, though it is considered debatable[cite: 1].

### CONS:
* [cite_start]Can be hard to understand, especially with complex problems[cite: 1].
* [cite_start]It can be hard to grasp the functionality[cite: 2].
* [cite_start]Potentially results in a large stack[cite: 1]. 
* [cite_start]Tail Call Optimization (TCO) can help manage stack size[cite: 1].
* [cite_start]Requires special techniques like memoization or dynamic programming to avoid exponential time complexity[cite: 1].

## Iteration
### PROS:
* [cite_start]Generally easier to understand[cite: 1].
* [cite_start]Typically utilizes a smaller call stack[cite: 1].

### CONS:
* [cite_start]Harder to avoid duplication[cite: 1].
* [cite_start]More verbose in its implementation[cite: 1].
